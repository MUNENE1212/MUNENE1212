## About

I’m an Electrical & Electronic Engineer and software builder focused on turning difficult, local problems into dependable products. My work spans business systems, artificial intelligence, climate intelligence, mobile payments, and embedded systems.

I’m especially interested in technology that works beyond the demo: on a low-bandwidth connection, on a mobile phone, through an M-Pesa flow, or on a real device in the field.

## How I build

- **Field-first:** product decisions start with the people, devices, networks, and payments that exist in the real environment.
- **Mobile-first:** experiences should work well on the phone most people already have.
- **Resilient by design:** offline-aware flows, useful fallbacks, and clear failure states matter.
- **Hardware-aware:** the best architecture respects both the cloud and the board at the edge.
- **Product-minded:** a technically impressive system is only valuable when it solves a real problem clearly.
